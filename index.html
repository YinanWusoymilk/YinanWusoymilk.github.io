Yinan Wu

PhD Student in Computer Science · North Carolina State University

Hi, I am Yinan Wu, 吴疑难. I study human-AI collaboration, especially how developers work with AI systems. My work sits at the intersection of Software Engineering and Human-Computer Interaction, using mixed-methods user studies to understand usability, trust, and cognition in AI-assisted programming.

Google Scholar LinkedIn CV (PDF)

Research

My research uses human-centered empirical methods to study AI-assisted software development. I design user studies, conduct usability evaluations, and apply mixed-methods analysis to translate developer workflow data into research insights.

I am fortunate to be co-advised by Dr. Bowen Xu and Dr. Kathryn Thomasset Stolee at NC State.

I am also a member of the SoftMax Lab and the Wolfpack Innovations in Software Engineering Research (WISER) group.

A quiet meaning in my name

My Chinese name is 吴疑难, pronounced Wú Yínán in Mandarin Chinese /u³⁵ i³⁵ nän³⁵/.

The small wordplay is that 吴 sounds like 无, meaning "no" or "without," while 疑难 means "difficulty" or "problem." So the name sounds like 无疑难: no difficulties.
